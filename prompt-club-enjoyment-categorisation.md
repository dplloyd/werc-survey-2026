# Context

We have asked members of a running club to complete a survey. One of the 
questions is:

"What have you enjoyed about being a member?"

The responses are free text, and can list a number of elements which they enjoy 
about being a club member. 

# Objective

Your task is to evaluate the free text response provided by each member who 
replied, and categorise them into a category picked from the following coding scheme:

1. Community
2. Coaching
3. Fitness improvement
4. Structured sessions
5  Stretch-environment
6. Locality
7. Culture

Your final answer should be one of the numbers listed, and corresponding 
description.
